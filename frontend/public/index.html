Our story

Honest pizza, made slowly.

Luna Pizza started with a simple idea: make honest pizza using good ingredients and traditional techniques.

The dough rests for two days before it ever meets the oven. The tomatoes are crushed by hand, the mozzarella is pulled fresh, and every pizza is baked over wood — the way they do it in Naples.

Whether it's a quick slice at the counter or a slow dinner with friends, you're always welcome at Luna.

Sofia & Marco — founders

Why it tastes good

Simple things, done well.

Fresh dough

Mixed every morning and rested for 48 hours. Never frozen, never rushed.

Italian ingredients

San Marzano tomatoes, fior di latte and olive oil from small growers.

Wood-fired oven

Baked at 850°F in ninety seconds for a light, blistered, smoky crust.
